I'm in my second year of medicine at Imperial. Most of what I do outside the course sits somewhere between medicine and software, which is a polite way of saying I get distracted by problems that look solvable.

I founded ImpulsiQ, an AI question generator that builds exam questions from a medical school's own curriculum and marks them with the reasoning behind every answer. It started because rereading notes and calling it revision was not working.
